# Reporting a problem

Something does not work, something could be better, or you have a question about Stamporama itself:
say so from where you are.

## From inside a collection

Choose **Report a problem** at the bottom of the sidebar (the speech-bubble icon when the sidebar is
folded). Pick what it is — a **problem**, an **idea** or a **question** — and write what you did,
what you expected and what happened.

Sent with your words, so you do not have to describe your own machine: the address of the screen
you are on, the app's version, your browser, the window size, your language and time zone, the
theme, and the last few script errors the page ran into, if any. Nothing from your collection is
attached.

## From a page shared with you

The pages a seller shares by link — an offer's specification, a proposal, a post's preview, a
choice of copies, a trade — carry a small **Report a problem** link at the bottom, in the page's
language. It needs no account. You may leave an email address if you want an answer; it is
optional.

The link in your address bar is private to you, so it is **not** stored with the report: only which
kind of page it was.

## Where reports go

To whoever runs the instance: they are listed in the [administrator's panel](admin.md#feedback-reports),
where each gets a status and, when it becomes one, a link to the issue tracking it.
