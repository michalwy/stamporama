# Filter presets

Every list with filters — Copies, Stamps, Issues, Wants, Offers, Proposals, Posts, Sales, Purchases,
Trades, Contacts, the auction watchlist and settlements, and Packing supplies — can keep the way you
have it set up under a name, and bring it back in one click. A preset of *PRL, for sale on Colnect,
\*\* only, grouped by issue* is set up once and then picked whenever you need it.

The **Presets** control sits beside the list's filters, right after the search box.

## What a preset holds

The **whole view of the list**:

- every filter, including the **area** and its *+ sub-areas* switch, the **years** and the
  **search** with the fields ticked beside it;
- the **sort**;
- the **grouping** and its switches (on Copies: grouping by duplicates, location or issue, and the
  condition, format and certificate splits).

Each list has its own presets — the Offers list never shows a Copies preset.

Presets belong to the **collection** and are kept on the server, so a preset saved on one computer is
there on every other device you sign in from.

## Saving, applying and changing presets

Open **Presets** and:

- **Save this view as…** — names the view on screen and saves it as a new preset. Names are unique
  on each list.
- **Pick a preset** to apply it. Applying **replaces** the whole view: every filter the preset does
  not hold is switched off, and the sort and grouping become the preset's. Nothing of what was on
  screen before is kept.
- The **pencil** beside a preset renames it; the **bin** deletes it (after asking). Deleting a preset
  leaves the view on screen as it is.

Once a preset is applied, its name is shown on the control. Change anything — a filter, the sort, the
grouping — and the control says **· modified**; **Update "…" with this view** then saves the change
into that preset.

To **stop using a preset**, press the **×** at the end of its chip, or pick **Stop using preset** in
the menu. The filters, sort and grouping stay exactly as they are; only the preset's name goes, and
the control reads **Presets** again. The preset itself is untouched and can be applied again.

Clearing every filter does the same by itself: once nothing narrows the list — no filter, no area,
no years, no search — the preset's name goes. The sort and the grouping do not count, so **Reset
filters** (with the area and years cleared) always brings you back to the plain list. With no preset
in use, **Save this view as…** is the way to keep the view.

Presets are listed by name. No preset is applied by itself: a list opens the way it always did, with
the filters it remembers from your last visit.

## When something a preset names is gone

A preset can name things that are later deleted — a tag, an area, a contact, a location, a platform
switched off for new offers, an archived Facebook group. Applying it then applies **everything else**
and a short note names what was left out, for example *Applied without tag "Rare" — no longer in the
collection.* Update the preset to stop seeing the note.
