# Filter presets

Every list with filters — Copies, Stamps, Issues, Wants, Offers, Proposals, Posts, Sales, Purchases,
Trades, Contacts, the auction watchlist and settlements, and Packing supplies — can keep the way you
have it set up under a name, and bring it back in one click. A preset of *for sale on Colnect, \*\*
only, grouped by issue* is set up once and then picked in whatever area you are working.

The **Presets** control sits beside the list's filters, right after the search box.

## What a preset holds

The **whole view of the list**, except the panel on its left:

- every filter, including the **search** with the fields ticked beside it;
- the **sort**;
- the **grouping** and its switches (on Copies: grouping by duplicates, location or issue, and the
  condition, format and certificate splits).

The **area**, its *+ sub-areas* switch and the **years** — everything chosen in the left panel — are
**not** part of a preset. They are where you are in the collection, and a preset applies to whatever
area and years you have chosen: applying one never moves the list to another area, and moving to
another area or other years never marks a preset *· modified*. Presets saved before this was so have
lost the area and years they held; applying one leaves the list where it is and sets everything else
as before.

Each list has its own presets — the Offers list never shows a Copies preset.

Presets belong to the **collection** and are kept on the server, so a preset saved on one computer is
there on every other device you sign in from.

## Saving, applying and changing presets

Open **Presets** and:

- **Save this view as…** — names the view on screen and saves it as a new preset. Names are unique
  on each list.
- **Pick a preset** to apply it. Applying **replaces** the view: every filter the preset does not
  hold is switched off, and the sort and grouping become the preset's. Only the area and the years
  stay as they were.
- The **pencil** beside a preset renames it; the **bin** deletes it (after asking). Deleting a preset
  leaves the view on screen as it is.

Once a preset is applied, its name is shown on the control. Change a filter, the sort or the grouping
and the control says **· modified**; **Update "…" with this view** then saves the change into that
preset — never the area or the years.

To **stop using a preset**, press the **×** at the end of its chip, or pick **Stop using preset** in
the menu. The filters, sort and grouping stay exactly as they are; only the preset's name goes, and
the control reads **Presets** again. The preset itself is untouched and can be applied again.

**Reset filters** (on some lists **Clear filters**) always lets go of the preset too, whatever the
preset held. The button clears what it always cleared and leaves the sort and the grouping on screen,
but the preset's name goes with the filters — it is never left on the control marked *· modified*,
and a reload does not bring it back.

Clearing the filters one by one does the same by itself, once nothing narrows the list — no filter
and no search. The sort, the grouping, the area and the years do not count. With no preset in use, **Save
this view as…** is the way to keep the view.

Presets are listed by name. No preset is applied by itself: a list opens the way it always did, with
the filters it remembers from your last visit.

## When something a preset names is gone

A preset can name things that are later deleted — a tag, a contact, a location, a platform
switched off for new offers, an archived Facebook group. Applying it then applies **everything else**
and a short note names what was left out, for example *Applied without tag "Rare" — no longer in the
collection.* Update the preset to stop seeing the note.
